Your bone and joint specialist in Sialkot

Dr. Muhammad Arif earned his MBBS at Nishtar Medical College, Multan (1998) and his Fellowship (FCPS) in Orthopedic Surgery from the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (2009). He trained at Mayo Hospital Lahore, Aga Khan University Hospital Karachi and Ghurki Trust Teaching Hospital Lahore.

He worked as a Specialist in Orthopedics at Armed Forces Hospital, Dhahran, Saudi Arabia, and has taught as Assistant and Associate Professor at Islam Medical College and Sialkot Medical College in Sialkot. He now serves as Associate Professor of Orthopedic Surgery at Akhtar Saeed Medical College, Rawalpindi, and performs both emergency and elective orthopedic surgery.

His advanced training includes shoulder arthroscopy (Aspetar, Doha), hip and knee arthroplasty (Lahore), Ilizarov external fixation and Advanced Trauma Life Support (2024). He has published research on fractures in adults and children.
